About Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli was born on 5 November 1988 in Delhi, India. From a young age, he showed a strong interest in cricket. With the support of his family, he joined the West Delhi Cricket Academy and began developing his cricketing skills.

He became well known after leading the Indian Under-19 cricket team to victory in the 2008 ICC Under-19 Cricket World Cup. His impressive performance helped him earn a place in the Indian senior cricket team.

Virat made his international debut for India in 2008. Through hard work, discipline and consistent performances, he established himself as one of the greatest batters in modern cricket.

He captained the Indian cricket team across formats and became known for his aggressive leadership, fitness, competitive attitude and match-winning performances.

Beyond cricket, Virat Kohli is admired for his fitness journey, charitable initiatives and influence on young athletes around the world.

Major Achievements

2011 World Cup

Was part of the Indian team that won the ICC Cricket World Cup in 2011.

2013 Champions Trophy

Played an important role in India's ICC Champions Trophy victory in 2013.

2018 Test Series

Led India to its first-ever Test series victory in Australia during the 2018–19 tour.

2023 World Cup

Scored a record 765 runs in the 2023 ICC Cricket World Cup and won Player of the Tournament.

2024 T20 World Cup

Played a match-winning role in India's 2024 ICC Men's T20 World Cup final victory.

IPL Champion 2025

Was part of Royal Challengers Bengaluru's historic maiden IPL title-winning team in 2025. He scored 657 runs in that season and the leading run-scorer to the team.

IPL Champion 2026

Was part of Royal Challengers Bengaluru's team that won their Back-to-Back IPL title in 2026. He scored 675 runs in that season and the leading run-scorer to the team. Played a match-winning role in the final against Gujarat Titans.

Personal Life

Virat Kohli married popular Bollywood actress Anushka Sharma on 11 December 2017 in a private wedding ceremony in Tuscany, Italy. Their relationship is popularly known as "Virushka". They first met during an advertisement shoot in 2013 and gradually developed a close relationship. Over the years, they have supported each other's professional careers and shared many important moments in their personal lives. Their relationship is admired by many fans across the world. Virat and Anushka have two children. Their daughter, Vamika Kohli, was born on 11 January 2021, and their son, Akaay Kohli, was born on 15 February 2024. The couple values family privacy and generally keeps their children away from excessive media attention. Virat often expresses the importance of family, love and maintaining a balance between professional responsibilities and personal life.
